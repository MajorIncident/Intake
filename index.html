        Which alerts, observations, measurements, reports, or reproducible results demonstrate the deviation?
        Which service, process, tool, machine, model, or configuration details distinguish the affected object?

        Baseline vs Current Behavior

        Which expected behavior, measure, or prior condition provides the normal baseline?
        Which current observations or measurements show how actual behavior differs from that baseline?

        Describe The Problem

        Impact

        What is the current impact?

        Who or what is affected now, and how large or severe is the impact?

        What future impact is likely if the issue remains unresolved?

        What downstream effect is likely if the deviation continues unresolved?

        By what specific deadline or timeframe does resolution become difficult, expensive, impossible, or meaningless?
