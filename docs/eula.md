## Scope
- Applies to anyone using the KT Intake web app to capture incident details, generate summaries, or export reports.
- Covers use of bundled templates, summary generators, and any future add-ons shipped with this site.
- The app runs in your browser. Shared collaboration and class features use a server to share work between participants (see Data usage). Your organization is responsible for meeting its own compliance requirements.

## Data usage
- **Working independently:** information you enter is stored only in your browser (localStorage key `kt-intake-full-v2`) until you clear it or export it. Nothing is sent to a server.
- **Collaboration and classes:** when you start or join a shared collaboration session, or join or run a class, the Intake you work on together is stored on our server so it can be shared with the other participants. This includes the Intake content, the display names you choose, team names, presence (who is currently viewing or editing), and, in classes, instructor coaching notes and exercise progress. It is used only for sharing. It stops being accessible automatically (by default 30 days after the shared session or class is created) and is permanently deleted when an administrator runs maintenance cleanup, which may also remove it earlier.
- Anyone who holds a collaboration link can read and edit that shared Intake. Share links only with the people who should see the work.
- Downloads you trigger (such as Save/Load files) stay on your device unless you choose to share them.
- If you connect the app to external services (e.g., via custom deployments), you are responsible for notifying users and following their terms.

## Restrictions

- Use the app in ways that respect privacy laws and organizational policies; disable integrations if you cannot ensure compliance.

## Export
- You may export summaries, checklists, and supporting notes for incident documentation and communication.
- Exports are provided "as is"; you must review and validate accuracy before acting on them.
- Removing your local data (clearing storage or deleting files) does not affect copies you have already shared externally.
